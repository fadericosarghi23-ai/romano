FONDAZIONE RAIMONDI – HOSPITAL

Gorla Minore, Varese
2016

This healthcare facility, mainly intended for rehabilitation, has been operating since 1909. The building consists of three floors with diagnosis and treatment facilities for patients with orthopedic problems or for those suffering from degenerative diseases. To improve its services and adapt it to the regulations in force, the healthcare facility had to be completely restored. The renovation was carried out in inpatient wards, outpatient departments, and administrative offices. Every department has clinics and functional services for the rooms each of which are equipped with
their own private bathroom suitable for disabled people. The sector dedicated to specialist clinics has been equipped with medical equipment and the necessary tools for diagnostics. The design choices were implemented on the basis of the best use of the environments in terms of functionality and comfort, while also enriching the spaces from an aesthetic point-of-view.

MILITARY BUILDINGS

2002 - Rho, Milan
2004 - Azzate, Varese
2004 - Cusano Milanese, Milano
2007 - Busto Arsizio, Varese
2010 - Pero, Milano

These projects were intended to create new police stations, consisting of headquarters and housing areas.The projects are divided into two parts. The first part consists of some administrative offices in addition to special units, while the second part concerns the accommodations for the police officers.
