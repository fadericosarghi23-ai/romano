Bio

Since the beginning in 1996, my architectural firm has been restoring, valorizing historic properties to their former glory and renovating several buildings in private and public sectors.

Diversity and diligence are fundamental features of my firm’s core belief. My firm is seasoned with challenges of working and execution of projects ranging from residential, office buildings, social housing and health care facilities. The firm has also carried out renovations for some military buildings, student dormitories and community centre, in collaboration with several Foundations in Milan.

Interior design is one of the field of  interest starting from finding the most articulated spaces which are best equipped to take advantage of available space and creation of new ones. Followed by careful material selection which compliments interior spaces and at the same time are harmonious.
