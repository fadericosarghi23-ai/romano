The projects for the internal renovation of homes and offices had as their purpose a reasonable redistribution of spaces and a search for the combination of shapes and colors. The arrangement of the interior spaces was combined with an attention to the aesthetic perception of the rooms. The continuous search for new materials to use, the application of shapes, and the use of colors allowed the improvement of the visual impact of the spaces and a stimulation of the senses in the search for the functions of every room. In the dwellings as well as in the work spaces, the choice of chromatic compositions and materials coordinated with each other was further developed through a single architectural expression.
